Term marks take 75% of your better term plus 25% of the other, scaled to 20. AAT is the average of both tests, scaled to 15. Attendance adds up to 5. That makes 40, and you pass above 20.

Term = (0.75 × better + 0.25 × other) × 20/30

AAT = average(AAT 1, AAT 2) × 15/10

Attendance: 75, 80, 85, 90% gives 2, 3, 4, 5

Total = Term + AAT + Attendance, capped at 40
